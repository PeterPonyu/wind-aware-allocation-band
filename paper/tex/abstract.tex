Target-position feedforward from a coarse disturbance estimate can change
whether a quadrotor finishes a waypoint inside a short episode. We map a
reproducible operating envelope for this information-to-completion interface
against a slow integral in a multi-quadrotor stack. A wind-agnostic arm uses
Euclidean assignment and the stock position controller; a wind-aware arm adds
an upwind assignment cost and target-position feedforward. Shared seeds, paired
contrasts and a seven-level force ladder define the primary envelope;
estimate-noise, temporal, pathway, geometry, swarm-size and capture-radius
campaigns extend the same endpoint. The envelope has a sharp transition at
\KneeLevel{}~N (\KneePctHover{}~percent of hover thrust), where completion
rises from \KneeAgnostic{} to \KneeAware{} (paired gain $+\KneeDelta{}$,
surviving the pre-specified signed-rank family correction). Doubling the
baseline integral gain collapses that \KneeLevel{}~N paired advantage to
\AuditKiTenGain{} (Wilcoxon \AuditKiTenWilcoxonP{}) and moves both arms'
transition to \AuditKiTenKnee{}~N. Initial assignment never differs from
Euclidean (\AuditInitialDivergence{} of \AuditAwareEpisodes{}) while replans
diverge in \AuditUsedDivergence{} episodes. Relative noise through
\BestSigma{} preserves the transition, and causal estimates retain positive
gains under constant and gusting profiles. A complete
assignment-by-feedforward factorial attributes the gain to target feedforward,
while paired gains stay positive from \ScaleMinSize{} through \ScaleMaxSize{}
airframes. Rerunning the ladder across capture radii keeps a transition of
comparable size but moves it from \RadiusTightPeakForce{} to
\RadiusWidePeakForce{}~N. The closer is therefore a scoped positive claim:
feedforward compensates a slow integral inside a \EpisodeHorizonS{}~s
episode, which locates the operating envelope rather than a general allocator
property.
